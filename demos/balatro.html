① State changes move volumes, not the playhead

Five full tracks start at the same moment and never stop. Every frame the desired track moves toward 1 by dt×3 and the others toward 0, a time constant of about 0.33 s. The melody keeps going across scene changes instead of restarting.

② Everything plays slow

All music in the game plays at per = 0.7: 30% slower and about 6.2 semitones lower. On Game Over, PITCH_MOD slides toward 0.5, about 63% of the way per second. That is the tape-stop you hear.

③ Your score adds layers

Organ volume is 0.1 × log₅(score / target), capped at 0.4, and only above the target. Fire follows the scoring-flame intensity, approximated here from the score ratio. Both layers sit outside the music and ignore PITCH_MOD.
