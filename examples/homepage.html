Homepage starter

A complete homepage in about 75 lines on top of src/endless-loop.js. Everything outside this panel is the template itself, exactly what a visitor sees. The dot on the right edge follows your mouse height, which sets the pitch bend.

You opened this file from disk. ES module imports are blocked on file://, so nothing will play. Run python3 -m http.server 8000 in the repo folder and open http://localhost:8000/examples/homepage.html, or use the live site.

How to take it

  1. Copy the .tpl styles, the main block and the module script from examples/homepage.html.
  2. Put src/endless-loop.js on your site.
  3. Or paste the prompt on the overview page into your AI tool.
